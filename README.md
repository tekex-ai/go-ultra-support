# Go Ultra! support

Help, bug reports and ideas for **Go Ultra!** and **Go Ultra! Lite**, the Garmin Connect IQ watch faces where an anime-style athlete powers up when you work out.

## Setting up the face
- **On the watch:** hold the watch face (or press and hold the menu button), then choose **Customize** to pick the character, difficulty and data fields.
- **On your phone:** Garmin Connect IQ app → *My Device* → *Go Ultra!* → *Settings*.

## FAQ
**A data field shows `--`.**
Your watch doesn't share that value with watch faces (for example, some models have no Training Status or Pulse Ox for faces), or it hasn't measured it yet.

**My workout isn't showing yet.**
The face reads your activity history a little after it wakes. Give it a few minutes after saving a workout.

**How does the power-up work?**
Workouts power up the character, in your sport's color. The difficulty decides how far you need to go:
- **Easy:** today only, up to level 4.
- **Hard:** builds a level each day you train, and drops one level per rest day.
- **Ultra:** builds like Hard, and drops one level every two rest days. An ultra-distance workout holds the top level for a week.
- Hard and Ultra are in the full app.

**Why does the character look beaten up?**
That's your Body Battery: the lower it is, the more worn out the character looks.

**I moved from Lite to the full app. Why did my progress reset?**
They're separate apps on your watch, so settings and power-up streaks don't carry over.
