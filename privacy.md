# Privacy policy: Go Ultra! and Go Ultra! Lite

*Effective: October 25, 2026*

**In short: the watch face doesn't collect, send or sell any of your data.**

## What the watch face uses
To draw the face, Go Ultra! reads data that is already on your Garmin watch:
- battery, steps, heart rate, calories, Body Battery and the other values you choose to show;
- your recent activities (sport, distance and time), to work out power-ups and your weekly distance;
- your profile's resting heart rate, and sunrise, sunset and weather from the watch.

All of this is processed **on your watch only**. The face has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. It saves only its settings and your power-up progress, on the watch.

## Garmin
Downloads, purchases and app crash reports go through Garmin's Connect IQ store and are covered by [Garmin's privacy policy](https://www.garmin.com/privacy/). Crash reports from Garmin show the developer only technical details (watch model, app version, where the app stopped), not who you are.

## When you contact us
If you email **tekex.ai@gmail.com** or open a GitHub issue, we see what you send, plus your email address or GitHub username. We use it only to answer you and improve the app, and we never sell or share it. GitHub issues are public, so don't post private details there. To have an email deleted, just ask.

## Children
The face isn't aimed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
